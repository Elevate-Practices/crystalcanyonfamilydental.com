Common questions.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. Kids, adults and grandparents are all welcome. Here's what to expect at your first visit, or book online in about a minute.

Do you take walk-in emergencies?

Yes. We offer same-day emergency care, no appointment necessary. Call (208) 454-0473 as early as you can. Emergency dental care.

I don't have dental insurance. Can you help?

Yes. The Smile Saver plan includes two cleanings, exams, X-rays and free whitening for $299 a year, plus 20% off restorative and cosmetic care. No deductibles, maximums or waiting periods.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in network with many dental insurance plans. Call (208) 454-0473 with your plan name and we'll check your benefits.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Se habla español. Our team can help you in English or Spanish.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 7:30 AM to 5 PM, and every other Friday, 7:30 AM to noon. Closed weekends.
