Dentures in Caldwell, ID

Full and partial dentures made to fit your mouth and bring back your smile and your bite.

Book online(208) 454-0473

Full or partial

  • Full dentures replace all the teeth on the top, bottom or both
  • Partial dentures fill gaps and clip to the teeth you still have
  • Implant-supported dentures snap onto implants for a secure fit

Made for your mouth

We take careful measurements so your dentures fit well, support your lips and cheeks, and look natural. We'll check the fit and make adjustments until they feel right.

Caring for your dentures

Brush them daily, soak them overnight and see us for regular checkups so we can keep them fitting well. Read how to take care of your dentures.

Common questions.

How long does it take to get dentures?

It usually takes a few visits for measurements, a try-in and the final fit.

Will dentures feel natural?

There's an adjustment period of a few weeks. We'll make adjustments so they feel comfortable.

Can you fix dentures that slip?

Often, yes. A reline or an implant-supported denture can help. Come in and we'll take a look.

Do you make partial dentures?

Yes. A partial fills gaps when you still have some healthy teeth.
