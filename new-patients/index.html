Your first visit

Here's what to expect.

Our goal is an enjoyable visit where you're an active part of your own dental health and confident in your treatment plan.

  1. 1

    Say hello.

    You'll meet our team and tell us about any concerns or past experiences at the dentist.

  2. 2

    A complete exam.

    A comprehensive oral exam that includes X-rays and photos of your teeth.

  3. 3

    Talk it through.

    Dr. Freeman goes over what he found with you. This is a great time to ask questions.

  4. 4

    Your plan.

    A treatment plan made for you, with costs and payment options shown before anything starts. No surprises.

Common questions.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take.

It's been years since my last visit. Is that okay?

Absolutely. We're glad you're here. We'll start where you are, with no lectures.

Do you see kids?

Yes, from first baby teeth on up. Family dentistry in Caldwell.

Can I get help in Spanish?

Yes. Se habla español.
