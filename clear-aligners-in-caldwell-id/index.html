How clear aligners work

Clear aligners are thin, see-through trays made for your teeth. You wear each set for a short time, and each one moves your teeth a little closer to where they belong. Most people won't notice you're wearing them.

Why people like them

  • Nearly invisible
  • Removable for eating, brushing and flossing
  • No metal brackets or wires
  • Fewer office visits than traditional braces

Are you a candidate?

Clear aligners work well for many cases of crowding, gaps and mild bite problems in adults and teens. Dr. Keven Freeman will look at your teeth and tell you honestly if aligners are a good fit.

Common questions.

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

It depends on how much your teeth need to move. Many cases take several months to about a year. We'll give you an estimate at your consult.

How many hours a day do I wear them?

About 20 to 22 hours a day. Take them out to eat, drink anything but water, and brush.

Are aligners good for teens?

They can be, if your teen will wear them consistently. We'll help you decide.

Can I get aligners if I have crowns or fillings?

Usually, yes. We'll check during your exam.
