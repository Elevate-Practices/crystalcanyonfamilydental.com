Common questions.

Is the Smile Saver plan dental insurance?

No. It's an in-office, reduced-fee savings plan. There's no insurance company, no claims and no approvals. You pay one yearly price, and your included care and discounts apply at your visits.

When can I start using it?

Right away. There are no waiting periods.

What's the difference between Dual and Family?

Dual covers two people. Family covers up to six family members, with children included until age 19. Additional family members are $95 each.

Can new patients join?

Yes. The plan is open to new and existing patients.

How do I sign up?

Give us a call at (208) 454-0473 or ask at your next visit and we'll get you set up.
