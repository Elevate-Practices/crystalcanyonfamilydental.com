One office for the whole family

Crystal Canyon Family Dental sees kids, teens, parents and grandparents. Many Caldwell families book back-to-back visits so it's one trip, not four. Dr. Keven Freeman and our hygienists keep things gentle and explain everything in plain language.

What happens at a checkup

  • A thorough cleaning that removes plaque and tartar from the places brushing misses
  • An exam of your teeth, gums and bite, including an oral cancer screening
  • Cavity-detecting X-rays when you need them
  • Honest answers, with your options and costs explained before any treatment

Kids are welcome here

From the first baby teeth to braces-age teens, we make visits easy and even fun. Want a head start at home? Read our tips on brushing teeth for kids and the baby teething timeline.

Comfort built in

Massage chairs, TVs on the ceiling and nitrous oxide for patients who feel nervous. Tell us how you feel about the dentist and we'll go at your pace.

Common questions.

How often should my family get cleanings?

Most people do best with a cleaning and exam every six months. If you have gum problems, we may suggest coming more often.

At what age should my child first see the dentist?

Around their first birthday or when the first tooth comes in. Early visits are short, friendly and help kids feel at home in the chair.

Can we book the whole family at once?

Yes. Call (208) 454-0473 and we'll line up back-to-back appointments when we can.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in network with many plans. Call (208) 454-0473 with your plan name and we'll check your benefits before your visit.
