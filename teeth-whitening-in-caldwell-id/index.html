Why professional whitening

Store-bought strips can be uneven and slow. Professional whitening from our office speeds up the process, gives you consistent, even shading and is safe for your enamel.

Who it's for

  • Stains from coffee, tea, soda or red wine
  • Teeth that have yellowed over time
  • Anyone getting ready for a wedding, photos or a big event
  • Patients finishing clear aligners

Before you whiten

Whitening works on natural teeth, not crowns or fillings. We'll check your teeth first and plan around any crowns you have so everything matches.

Common questions.

Does teeth whitening hurt?

Some people have mild, short-lived sensitivity. We'll suggest ways to keep you comfortable.

How long does whitening last?

Results can last a long time with good habits. Cutting back on coffee, tea and soda helps.

Is whitening really free?

Yes, for Smile Saver members and new patients who book our $99 new-patient visit.

Will whitening change my crowns?

No. Whitening only lightens natural teeth, so we'll plan around any crowns or fillings.
